My trades on chart

Drop your Zerodha tradebook files below to see every trade you have made plotted on that stock's price chart, with your average cost, realized profit and holding periods.
Your data stays on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.

Drop tradebook CSV files here or click to choose them · several years at once is fine
Where to get the files
  1. Open console.zerodha.com → Reports → Tradebook.
  2. Pick segment Equity, set a date range, and download as CSV. Zerodha allows one year per file, so download one per year.
  3. Drop all of them here together. Overlapping ranges are fine, repeats are ignored.
